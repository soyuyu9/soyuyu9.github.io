I am an elementary school teacher in South Korea and a researcher in democratic education. I study how teachers exercise political agency under norms of neutrality: when they speak, when they stay silent, and what that means for civic education and educational justice.

Research interests: teacher agency, voice, and dissent · political neutrality and democratic education · teacher demoralization and professional ethics · civic inequality and educational justice
